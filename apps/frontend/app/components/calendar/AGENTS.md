# Schedule & Calendar — Agent Instructions

Loaded with the [frontend guide](../../../AGENTS.md); its § numbers are referenced here. Edit here, not there.

- **Schedule:** `schedule.tsx` composes `use-schedule-*` hooks, `ScheduleCalendar` and `ScheduleOverlays`; `use-schedule-data` reads the active month/week/day/year range; holidays reuse cached years for 7 days per country, subdivision and locale. Compact shells change period by dragging the calendar body (`hooks/ui/use-period-swipe.ts`) through the same path the header arrows use. Trash on a recurring block uses whole-series wording; Undo restores the task and reopens its details through `useTaskDetailsRequest`. Habit logs hydrate into **virtual habit tasks** on some schedule surfaces — intentional hybrid behavior, don't simplify away. **Phones** (`components/calendar/phone/`) zoom Day → Month → Year (no Week; `?view=week` shows Day, which fetches the whole week): `DayStrip` (`day-<iso>` drop targets) over `DayAgenda`, and `MonthFold`. Rows are `ScheduleRow`, grouped by kind in one place (`lib/utils/calendar/schedule-day.ts`, routines included); swipe right completes, left offers Tomorrow / Pick day, then a clock and **4:30 PM** (Later today; the tray measures itself, never wraps; `useLaterToday`: the first gap from now, for an untimed task or a block whose time has passed, only when today has one; it never moves the first two). Clutter toggles are per kind (`calendar.clutter.showFixed` etc.); Day and Week open at `calendar.timelineStartHour` (default 7). Desktop Month folds repeating Fixed blocks into one line per cell (`splitMonthDay`). **Desktop Week/Day:** `TimedCalendarColumn` shares their grid, drop previews and Task/Event context creation; the query, header and columns share the settings-derived week start. `buildTimedTaskLayouts` lays routines out as points that never take column width (`RoutineRow`, or a `RoutineBadge` docked on the block they overlap); anytime routines are a strip of ring marks with a visible details/overflow control under the all-day lane (`AllDayWeekLane`, multi-day items are one bar). Blocks use size containers (`.cal-chip` in `app.css`); chips show one kind mark (task checkbox · routine ring · none for Fixed), and checks off, opens, and offers its preview/menu through `CalendarItem` with one fade/highlight policy (`CalendarChipActionsContext`); Trash lives in that menu and the panel, never on the chip. Hovering a chip shows a read-only card and lights its series (`CalendarHoverContext`). A routine opens `EditSidePanel kind="habit"` in place on every shell, with the clicked day's times; `?routine=` on `/routines` selects one. Laptop moves ‹ Today › to `PeriodDock` (bottom right) on Schedule and Routines; the assistant is the icon rail's, with no floating launcher.
