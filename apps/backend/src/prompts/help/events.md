# Events
- [Events](/events) holds personal events like birthdays and anniversaries. Use Add event to create one, or ask the assistant to add, change or remove one.
- Each event can have an emoji, a colour (a soft tint on its card), a reminder on the day, and a start date so it counts the years.
- Whether yearly events show in Schedule is the Yearly events switch under Show on calendar in [Calendar & Time](?settings=datetime).
- Holidays for your location come from [Location & Weather](?settings=location).
- You can share a yearly date by link from its menu (**Share…**). Anyone with the link can add it to their events and both of you can change it. **Remind me** is yours alone, and anyone it's shared with can choose **Remove from my events** anytime.
