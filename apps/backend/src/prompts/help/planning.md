# Planning
- [Today](/today): fixed blocks, timed routines, Still open carry-overs, today's tasks and routines, in one place.
- [Schedule](/schedule): day, week, month and year views (e.g. [this week](/schedule?view=week)). Drag tasks onto it to schedule them.
- **Fit into today's gaps** (in Today's header controls): lays today's untimed tasks that have a known length into the gaps between your blocks, in Today's order, as a preview. Drag or remove any, pick a length for the ones without, then Place; one Undo puts them all back. Timed tasks and Fixed blocks never move. Ask the assistant to plan your day for the same preview.
- [Upcoming](/upcoming): what's coming up after today: the next 7 days, then every later task folded under Further out.
- [Weekly Reset](/weekly-review): an optional look back and plan ahead, step by step.
- **Focus views:** Focus on Today, Upcoming, a list or Capture filters tasks (list, tags, priority, effort, due window) with presets or a typed phrase; save one to reuse it. The assistant can show, save, change or delete them.
- Shortcuts: `G` then `T` Today, `S` Schedule, `U` Upcoming, `W` Weekly Reset.
