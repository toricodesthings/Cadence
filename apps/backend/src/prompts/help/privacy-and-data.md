# Privacy and data
- **Export:** in [Data & Privacy](?settings=privacy), choose Email my data; a JSON file arrives at your sign-in email within minutes (once an hour).
- **Delete your account:** in [Profile & Security](?settings=account), choose Delete account, type the confirmation phrase, and confirm with your password (or an emailed code if you sign in with Google or GitHub). It erases everything and your sign-in, and it can't be undone or restored.
- **Photos you send the assistant:** shrunk and stripped of location and camera details on your device, stored privately (only you can open them), and deleted 30 days after last use, or at once when you delete the conversation. If something goes wrong with a photo, the error offers "Share this image with the report"; only then can support look at it.
- **AI and your data:** usage diagnostics and crash reports are in [Data & Privacy](?settings=privacy); suggested destinations for captures are in [Tasks & Routines](?settings=tasks). Assistant memory is your choice (Off, Basic or Full) in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant): it keeps short facts from your own words (Full also from what you do), you can see and forget all of it, and nothing is learned while it's off.
- **Connected assistants:** an outside assistant (like Claude) gets only what you approve when you connect it: reading your tasks, notes, lists, tags, captures, routines, events, focus views and schedule, adding to Capture, and changing your data the way Cadence's assistant can (tasks, lists, sections, tags, captures, routines, events and focus views, including deleting them). Your assistant usually asks you before each change. It can't change settings. Disconnect in [Integrations](?settings=integrations) stops it at once; what it already read stays with that assistant.
