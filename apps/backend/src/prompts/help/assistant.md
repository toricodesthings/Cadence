# The assistant
- **Can:** read photos you send (a whiteboard, a syllabus, a screenshot) and turn what they show into tasks; read your tasks, notes, schedule, routines, events, captures, lists, tags and focus views; add tasks (several at once, with checklists, repeats, Fixed blocks, reminders and tags by name), copy, reorder, change or move tasks, set a Waiting check-in or hide a task until a day, edit notes, add, tick off, reorder or remove subtasks; make, rename, recolour or delete lists and tags; add, rename, reorder or delete sections and sort tasks into them; create, change, pause, reorder, archive, log or delete routines; add, change or remove events (with an emoji); sort captures into tasks, keep, tick off, discard, restore or delete them; save, change or delete focus views; plan or lighten a day or week and undo its last change; explain Cadence and link you to places. A note edit shows exactly what changes first, and a long note can only be added to. After you approve, it keeps going, so "make a list and put these in it" happens in one go.
- **Can't (yet):** change settings; read PDFs or other files.
- **Questions:** when a detail isn't clear (which task, which time, what happens to a list's tasks), the assistant asks instead of guessing: tap one of its options or type your own answer in the box, then send.
- **Photos:** attach with the + under the chat box, paste or drop them in: up to 4 per message and 20 a day (the + shows how many are left when you're close, and when the count resets). A photo is kept 30 days after it was last sent, then shows "Image expired"; send it again if you need it.
- **Limits and commands:** type /usage in the chat box to see how much is left in the 5-hour and weekly limits and today's photos, and when each resets (also at the bottom of [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant)). Type /clear for a fresh conversation (the old one stays in history; to always start fresh after opening Cadence, turn off Continue last conversation in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant)), /history for past ones, /memory for what Cadence knows about you, and /settings for the assistant's settings. The / button next to the photo button lists them all. None of these sends a message.
- **Approval mode** (the menu under the chat box): Ask first (you approve every change), Auto (changes apply themselves, except permanent deletes, removing subtasks, rewriting a whole note, and anything touching more than 5 tasks or captures). Task destination cards show the list and section. On a card with several rows, untick any you don't want and the assistant offers the rest again, Full (everything applies, permanent deletes included).
- **Name, voice and more** live in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant): the assistant's name, your nickname, the voice (Secretary, Coach, Minimalist, Companion), emoji, suggestions, adaptive tone, memory and custom instructions.
- **Memory:** the first time you open the assistant it asks how much to remember: Basic (what you tell it), Full (also what you do: what you finish and move, how long things take, your task notes), or off. Say "remember…" to save something at once, or "forget…" to drop it; both show a line with Undo. Open **What Cadence knows** (the brain button in the assistant's header, or /memory) to see everything in plain sentences, tap an item to forget it, or tell the assistant what to change. Choosing Full also reads your last 3 months of chats and notes, a little each night; What Cadence knows shows a bar while it reads and what it learned when it's done. "Don't learn from this chat" is in a conversation's menu in History. Change the choice any time in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant).
- How your data is used for AI is under [Intelligence & Privacy](?settings=ai).
