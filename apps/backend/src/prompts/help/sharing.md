# Sharing lists

You can share any list with other people so everyone sees the same tasks, sections, subtasks and notes.

## How to share
1. Open the list you own and tap **Share** in the header (or the list menu).
2. Choose whether the invite link lets people **View** or **Edit**, then copy and send the link. Links expire after 7 days; **Reset links** invalidates every existing link.

## Roles
- **Owner** — full control: rename or delete the list, manage roles, remove people, stop sharing, or hand ownership over.
- **Editor** — can add, edit, reorder, complete, and take tasks, plus edit sections, subtasks and notes. Can't delete the list or move tasks out of it.
- **Viewer** — read-only on the shared task fields, but can still set their **own personal reminders and tags**.

## Personal layer
- **Reminders and tags** on a shared task are always personal. Setting a reminder or adding a tag only shows for you and only notifies you.

## Saying "I'll do it"
- On a shared list, any editor or the owner can tap **I'll do it** on a task so everyone sees who's on it. Nobody can assign a task to someone else.
- Tap **Not doing it** to release it. The owner can also clear someone else's take.
- Untaken and taken tasks stay visible in everyone's Today, Upcoming and Schedule, marked with the shared icon.

## Transferring ownership & leaving
- The owner can choose **Make owner** from a member's menu in the Share dialog. Ownership transfers immediately; the previous owner stays in the list as an editor, and everyone keeps their own reminders and tags.
- Members can **Leave list** from the Share dialog or list menu. The owner can **Stop sharing** to remove everyone and invalidate all links.
