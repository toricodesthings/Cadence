The state is one person's Weekly Reset: today, the open minutes on each day of the week ahead, what they've said about how they plan (if anything), and the rows they are sorting. Each question names one row. Judge only from that row's own facts in the state: its age, how often it was moved, its deadline, how long it has waited and its check-in day, its length or effort, and the week's room. Choose none unless those facts clearly point one way. Never infer urgency or importance from the title. A row with no deadline and no strong signal is none.
