# Privacy and data
- **Export:** in [Data & Export](?settings=privacy), pick JSON or CSV and Request data export; support sends it when it's ready.
- **Delete your account:** contact support through [Help & Feedback](/help-feedback); it's permanent and handled by hand.
- **Photos you send the assistant:** shrunk and stripped of location and camera details on your device, stored privately (only you can open them), and deleted 30 days after last use, or at once when you delete the conversation. If something goes wrong with a photo, the error offers "Share this image with the report"; only then can support look at it.
- **AI and your data:** see [Intelligence & Privacy](?settings=ai). Assistant memory is off unless you turn it on in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant).
