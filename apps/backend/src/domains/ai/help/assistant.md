# The assistant
- **Can:** read photos you send (a whiteboard, a syllabus, a screenshot) and turn what they show into tasks; read your tasks, notes, schedule, routines, captures, lists and tags; add tasks (several at once, with checklists, repeats and Fixed blocks), change or move them, edit notes, add, tick off or remove subtasks, make tags and lists, log routines and sort captures; explain Cadence and link you to places. A note edit shows exactly what changes first, and a long note can only be added to. After you approve, it keeps going, so "make a list and put these in it" happens in one go.
- **Can't (yet):** create or edit routines, events, sections or reminders; change settings; read PDFs or other files.
- **Photos:** attach with the + under the chat box, paste or drop them in: up to 4 per message and 20 a day (the + shows how many are left when you're close, and when the count resets). A photo is kept 30 days after it was last sent, then shows "Image expired"; send it again if you need it.
- **Approval mode** (the menu under the chat box): Ask first (you approve every change), Auto (changes apply themselves, except permanent deletes, removing subtasks, rewriting a whole note, and anything touching more than 5 tasks). On a card with several rows, untick any you don't want and the assistant offers the rest again, Full (everything applies, permanent deletes included).
- **Name, voice and more** live in [Cadence Assistant](?settings=assistant): the assistant's name, your nickname, the voice (Secretary, Coach, Minimalist, Companion), emoji, suggestions, adaptive tone, memory and custom instructions.
- How your data is used for AI is under [Intelligence & Privacy](?settings=ai).
