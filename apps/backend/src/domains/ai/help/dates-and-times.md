# Dates and times
- **A day or a time block:** a task sits on a day (all-day, or with that day as its deadline) or takes a time slot with a start and end. A deadline is a day; for a time of day, plan a time block or set a reminder.
- **Typing dates:** write "tomorrow 3pm" or "fri" in a title and Cadence fills the date. Tune how eager it is under [Tasks & Workflow](?settings=tasks).
- **Dragging:** drag a task onto a day or time in [Schedule](/schedule?view=week) to schedule it.
- **Overdue:** only tasks can be overdue. Unfinished tasks carry over to [Today](/today) under Still open, without fuss.
- Time zone (Device follows your device; or pin one), first day of the week and date format live in [Calendar & Time](?settings=datetime).
