# Dates and times
- **Due vs scheduled:** *due* is when it matters; *scheduled* is when you plan to do it. Both are optional.
- **All-day vs time block:** a task can sit on a day or take a time slot with a start and end.
- **Typing dates:** write "tomorrow 3pm" or "fri" in a title and Cadence fills the date. Tune how eager it is under [Tasks & Workflow](?settings=tasks).
- **Dragging:** drag a task onto a day or time in [Schedule](/schedule?view=week) to schedule it.
- **Overdue:** only tasks can be overdue. Unfinished tasks carry over to [Today](/today) under Still open, without fuss.
- Timezone, first day of the week and date format live in [Calendar & Time](?settings=datetime).
