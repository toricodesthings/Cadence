# Repeats
Three kinds, told apart by one question: **if you miss one, what happens?**
- **It just passes → Fixed** (class, shift). Never overdue, never checked off. Needs a start and end time.
- **Let it go → Routine** (gym, stretch). Done or skipped per day; lives in [Routines](/routines).
- **Still owed → repeating Task** (rent). Carries over until done.

**Make one:**
1. A routine: Quick Add (`T`) → the Routine tab, pick how often it repeats (daily or every few days, Mon–Fri, Sat–Sun, or custom days, weekly or every other week), then Create routine.
2. A repeating task: open the task's date picker and choose Repeat (daily, weekdays, weekly, biweekly or monthly).
3. To switch kinds, open the repeating item and answer "If you miss one…": Still owed, Let it go, or It just passes.
