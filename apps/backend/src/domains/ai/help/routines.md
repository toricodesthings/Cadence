# Routines
- Routines live in [Routines](/routines) and show on [Today](/today). Timed ones appear on the [Schedule](/schedule). The assistant can create routines and change, pause or archive them.
- [Routines](/routines) opens on today: tap a routine to check it in. Below it, the week (or month) shows every day.
- Any past day can be logged, from the week, the month, or a routine's History. Click or tap a day to mark it done; right-click or press and hold for Done, Skip or Clear. Skipping is neutral.
- Each routine can have an emoji and a colour (in its Details), and a purpose (in its Notes).
- **Steps:** a routine can hold a short sequence (water → stretch → journal), added when you create it (under More) or in its Steps section. A day shows how many are settled (2/3). Tapping the day still checks off every step; press and hold (or right-click), or open the routine, to tick or skip steps one by one. The day counts as done once every step is done or skipped.
- **Pause** (beside Archive, at the bottom of a routine) for 3 days, a week, two weeks or until a date; days already logged stay. **Archive routine** puts it away (archived routines can be restored).
- Streaks are quiet; turn them off from the gear on the Routines page, or with Routine streaks in [Tasks & Workflow](?settings=tasks).
- Routine reminders are in [Notifications](?settings=notifications); a reminder only comes on a day the routine is due and still open.
