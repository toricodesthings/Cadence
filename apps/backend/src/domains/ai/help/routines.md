# Routines
- Routines live in [Routines](/routines) and show on [Today](/today). Timed ones appear on the [Schedule](/schedule).
- [Routines](/routines) opens on today: tap a routine to check it in. Below it, the week (or month) shows every day.
- Any past day can be logged, from the week, the month, or a routine's History. Click or tap a day to mark it done; right-click or press and hold for Done, Skip or Clear. Skipping is neutral.
- Each routine can have an emoji and a colour (in its Details).
- **Pause** for 3 days, a week, two weeks or until a date; days already logged stay. **Archive routine** puts it away (archived routines can be restored).
- Streaks are quiet; turn them off in the Routines page's Controls, or with Routine streaks in [Tasks & Workflow](?settings=tasks).
- Routine reminders are in [Notifications](?settings=notifications); a reminder only comes on a day the routine is due and still open.
