# Routines
- Routines live in [Routines](/routines) and show on [Today](/today). Timed ones appear on the [Schedule](/schedule).
- Each day, mark a routine done or skip it. Skipping is neutral.
- **Pause for 7 days** when life gets busy; **Archive routine** to put it away (archived routines can be restored).
- Streaks are quiet; turn them off with Routine streaks in [Tasks & Workflow](?settings=tasks).
- Routine reminders and missed-routine prompts are in [Notifications](?settings=notifications).
