# Shortcuts
- `T` Quick Add · `Q` capture · `/` search · `Ctrl/Cmd+K` command palette · `?` shortcut reference.
- On a selected task: `C` complete, `E` move to Trash.
- `G` then `T` Today, `S` Schedule, `I` Capture, `H` Routines, `U` Upcoming, `W` Weekly Reset.
- Rebind keys or turn shortcuts off in [Keyboard Shortcuts](?settings=shortcuts).
