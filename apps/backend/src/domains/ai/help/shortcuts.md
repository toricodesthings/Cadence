# Shortcuts
- `T` Quick Add · `Q` capture · `/` search · `Ctrl/Cmd+K` command palette · `?` shortcut reference.
- On a selected task: `C` complete, `E` move to Trash.
- `G` then `T` Today, `S` Schedule, `I` Capture, `H` Routines, `U` Upcoming, `W` Weekly Reset.
- Rebind keys or turn shortcuts off in [Keyboard Shortcuts](?settings=shortcuts).
- On a focused [Capture](/) row: Up/Down or J/K moves focus; 1 Today, 2 Tomorrow, 3 No day yet, X tick off, Enter details, Backspace discard, Space select. Hints appear on the focused row with a fine pointer.
- In Capture's field, Enter saves; Shift+Enter inserts a newline; Ctrl/Cmd+Enter saves as a task.
